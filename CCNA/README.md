\# Cisco Networking Labs



Hands-on Cisco networking labs completed as part of my preparation for the \*\*CCNA (200-301)\*\* certification.



This repository documents my practical work with \*\*Cisco IOS, Ethernet switching, IPv4, ARP, MAC address learning, network traffic analysis, device security, and network troubleshooting\*\*.



The labs are based on the \*\*Jeremy's IT Lab CCNA course\*\* and are used as a practical environment for developing and documenting my networking skills.



> The lab scenarios and exercises are based on the course material. The configurations, verification, analysis, and documentation represent my own work.
